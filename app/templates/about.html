About StudentCare

StudentCare connects students, volunteer organizations, and the Campus Volunteerism Centre to make community service easier to discover, manage, and recognize.

For Students

Find volunteer opportunities, apply to projects, keep track of active volunteering, and submit hours for verification. Earn credits for approved hours and redeem them for rewards.

For Volunteer Organizations

Share opportunities with students by posting project listings, then manage your listings and view applicants. The Campus Volunteerism Centre reviews listings and makes application decisions.

For the Campus Volunteerism Centre

Review project listings and student applications, verify submitted volunteer hours, and keep track of student participation and resignations.
